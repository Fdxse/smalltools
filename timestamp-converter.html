Timestamp

Auto picks the unit from the number of digits: up to 11 is seconds, 12 to 14 millis, 15 and 16 micros, 17 and up nanos. Dates look like 2026-11-05 14:30 or Thu, 05 Nov 2026 14:30:00 GMT. A date with no zone of its own is read in the time zone above. Results are shown to the millisecond.
